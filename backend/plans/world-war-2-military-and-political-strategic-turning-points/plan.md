## Phase 2: Early Military Turning Points

- [ ] The Battle of Britain (1940) — Examining the Luftwaffe's failure to achieve air superiority over Britain. Understanding the impact on delaying and ultimately preventing Operation Sea Lion (German invasion of Britain). Highlighting the significance of radar and British fighter pilot skill.
- [ ] Operation Barbarossa (1941) — Analyzing the initial successes of the German invasion of the Soviet Union. Assessing how strategic miscalculations, logistical problems, and underestimation of Soviet resistance ultimately stalled the offensive and opened the Eastern Front.
- [ ] The Attack on Pearl Harbor (1941) — Detailing the attack and its immediate consequences. Analyzing its strategic impact: bringing the United States into the war and shifting the balance of power against the Axis powers. Understanding the miscalculations of the Japanese regarding American resolve.
- [ ] The Battle of Moscow (1941-1942) — Understanding the Soviet Union's successful defense of Moscow. Analyzing the effects of the harsh winter conditions and the redirection of Soviet troops on halting the German advance. Assessing the psychological impact of this first major defeat on the Germans.

## Phase 3: Mid-War Military and Political Shifts

- [ ] The Battle of Stalingrad (1942-1943) — Examining the brutal urban warfare and the eventual encirclement and surrender of the German Sixth Army. Analyzing the battle's significance as a major turning point on the Eastern Front, shifting momentum in favor of the Soviet Union.
- [ ] The Second Battle of El Alamein (1942) — Examining the British victory in North Africa under General Montgomery. Understanding its strategic importance in securing the Suez Canal, controlling Middle Eastern oil reserves, and preventing Axis forces from linking up in the Middle East.
- [ ] The Casablanca Conference (1943) — Analyzing the agreements made between Roosevelt and Churchill, including the demand for unconditional surrender of the Axis powers. Discussing the political implications of this decision on the future conduct of the war and post-war settlement.
- [ ] The Battle of Kursk (1943) — Covering the last major German offensive on the Eastern Front. Analyzing its strategic importance as the largest tank battle in history, and the Soviet Union’s demonstrated ability to withstand and defeat the German offensive. Assessing its impact on the overall balance of power on the Eastern Front.

## Phase 4: Late-War Turning Points & Political Realignments

- [ ] The Invasion of Sicily (1943) — Detailing the Allied invasion of Sicily and its subsequent liberation. Discussing its impact on the collapse of the Mussolini regime in Italy. Analyzing its strategic importance in opening up the Mediterranean and preparing for the invasion of mainland Italy.
- [ ] Operation Overlord (D-Day, 1944) — Covering the Allied landings in Normandy and the subsequent liberation of France. Understanding its strategic importance as the opening of the Western Front and the beginning of the end for Nazi Germany.
- [ ] The Yalta Conference (1945) — Analyzing the agreements made between Roosevelt, Churchill, and Stalin regarding the post-war order in Europe and Asia. Discussing the political implications of these agreements, including the division of Germany, the establishment of Soviet influence in Eastern Europe, and the Soviet commitment to enter the war against Japan.
- [ ] The Potsdam Conference (1945) — Analyzing the agreements of the Allied powers and the changing dynamics of the alliance especially with the US President Truman. Discussing the final decisions on Germany, the warning to Japan, and the growing tensions between the Soviet Union and the Western Allies.
