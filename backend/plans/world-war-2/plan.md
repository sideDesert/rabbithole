# World War 2

> **Depth:** overview | **Prior knowledge:** Some experience watching documentaries. Wants to learn about the political and social context of the war, not specific battles. Wants a broad-strokes understanding.

## Phase 1: Seeds of Conflict

- [ ] Treaty of Versailles — An overview of the treaty's terms and its impact on Germany, including territorial losses, reparations, and the "war guilt" clause.
- [ ] Rise of Fascism and Nazism — Examination of the political and economic conditions contributing to the rise of fascist ideologies in Italy and Nazism in Germany, emphasizing key figures like Mussolini and Hitler.
- [ ] Global Economic Depression — The impact of the Great Depression on international relations, including protectionism and the destabilization of democracies.
- [ ] Appeasement — Analysis of the policy of appeasement adopted by Britain and France towards Germany, including the motivations behind it and its consequences.
- [ ] Japanese Expansionism — An overview of Japan's imperial ambitions in Asia, including the invasion of Manchuria and its growing militarism.

## Phase 2: The War and its Broad Impact

- [ ] Major Alliances — Understanding the main alliances: the Axis powers (Germany, Italy, Japan) and the Allied powers (Britain, France, United States, Soviet Union), and how they formed.
- [ ] Key Theaters of War — A high-level overview of the major theaters: European, Pacific, and North African, without going into specific battle details.
- [ ] The Holocaust — An introduction to the systematic persecution and murder of Jews and other minority groups by the Nazi regime, emphasizing its scale and ideological underpinnings.
- [ ] Role of Technology — Discussion of how technological advancements (e.g., radar, codebreaking, airplanes, tanks) influenced the war's course and outcomes.
- [ ] Propaganda and Public Opinion — How governments manipulated information and shaped public opinion in their respective countries to support the war effort.

## Phase 3: Aftermath and Legacy

- [ ] The Atomic Bombings — Examination of the decision to use atomic bombs on Hiroshima and Nagasaki, including the ethical considerations and the immediate and long-term consequences.
- [ ] Formation of the United Nations — The UN's creation as an attempt to prevent future global conflicts, and its initial goals and structure.
- [ ] The Cold War Begins — How WW2's end planted the seeds for a new geopolitical conflict between the United States and the Soviet Union.
- [ ] Decolonization — The acceleration of decolonization movements in Asia and Africa following the war, due to European powers being weakened and new ideologies emerging.
- [ ] Economic Restructuring — The establishment of new international financial institutions, such as the World Bank and the International Monetary Fund, to promote global economic stability.
